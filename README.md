# HRM Office

HRM Office helps organizations understand the skills their people have and plan the skills they need. It brings employee information, competency assessments, job expectations, and workforce insights together in one place.

## What it helps you do

- Keep employee and team information organized by department.
- Define the competencies and proficiency levels important to your organization.
- Set the skills expected for different roles and jobs.
- Let employees assess themselves and let assessors provide their own evaluations.
- Review assessment differences together and agree on a final view of each employee's skills.
- See individual and organization-wide skill gaps to guide development and staffing decisions.
- Record performance appraisals and follow progress over time.

## Built for the whole team

HR administrators manage the organization, team members, departments, roles, and competency framework. Assessors evaluate employees and contribute to assessment reviews. Employees complete their self-assessments and can see information relevant to their own development.

Each organization has its own workspace, so HR teams can manage their people and assessment information in one place.

## The goal

HRM Office turns assessment results into a clearer picture of workforce strengths and development needs. That helps teams make more informed decisions about learning, role readiness, and future capability.
